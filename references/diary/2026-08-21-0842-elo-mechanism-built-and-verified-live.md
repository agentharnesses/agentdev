Worth recording in more than "it ran": step 1's blind judge reasoning, with zero access to any rubric or to this session's own prior investigation, independently re-derived the exact `ahar validate` (CLI wrapper) vs. `harnesses_ref/validator.py` (underlying implementation) distinction traced by hand earlier today (`2026-08-21`, the "why is end accuracy so low" exchange) — correctly favoring answers that named the concrete module/functions over answers that stopped at the CLI layer, and correctly identifying which of a variant's own repeat answers *disagreed* with each other on that same distinction for the consistency comparisons.
